Privacy Policy – Better Tomorrow 🌍

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Better Tomorrow 🌍 is an Android application designed to provide useful everyday information and tools.

1. Location Information

When you choose to use location-dependent features and grant Android location permission, the app may access your approximate or precise device location.

Location information may be used to provide weather information and nearby or map-related functionality. The app does not use background location access.

2. Internet Connection

The app requires an internet connection to provide some features. When the app communicates with third-party services, those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and standard connection metadata according to their own privacy policies.

3. How Location Information Is Used

Location access is requested when you choose a feature that requires it. Better Tomorrow 🌍 does not require you to create an account and does not maintain a personal location-history database on its own server.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the feature you use, the app may communicate with third-party services, including:

  • Open-Meteo – weather information
  • Frankfurter – currency exchange information
  • ESPN – sports information
  • Google News RSS – news information
  • REST Countries – country information
  • Wikipedia – information content
  • USGS – earthquake information
  • Google Maps and Google Search – maps, places and search-related functionality

These third-party services may process technical information or information included in requests according to their own privacy policies.

5. Information We Do Not Request

Better Tomorrow 🌍 does not require an account and does not request your name, phone contacts, passwords, SMS messages, or payment-card information for the core app functionality described in this policy.

6. Data Retention

Better Tomorrow 🌍 does not maintain a user account database or personal location-history database on its own server. Location information used for a current feature is not intended to be retained as a personal location history by the app.

Third-party services may retain technical request information according to their own policies.

7. Security

The app uses internet connections and HTTPS where supported by the relevant service. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

Better Tomorrow 🌍 is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Privacy Contact

For privacy questions concerning Better Tomorrow 🌍, please use the developer contact email displayed on the application's Google Play Store listing and identify the app as Better Tomorrow 🌍.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at this same webpage.

11. Your Choices

You can grant or revoke Android location permission through your device settings. If location permission is denied, features that require location information may not function correctly.

12. Consent

By using features that require location permission, you consent to the processing described in this Privacy Policy and the Android permission system. You may withdraw location permission through your device settings.
